# Anti-Victory: RULED BY NO-ONE
Histories are told; written; woven. What is created by the Hours for the Hours, and I chose instead to unmake their designs. I make myself beholden to nothing, so it is Nowhere to which I belong. This is the place where everything finds its end, as the droplet ends in the lake. This is the place no-one rules, where I obey no-one, and capitulate to no-one’s demands. No-One pulls at my threads to weave this endless domain. Here lies the freedom so long denied. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper into darkness.

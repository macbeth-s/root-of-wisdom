# Anti-Victory: SHADOW'S MERCY
When the House of the Moon is opened, Nowhere is swept into its halls like a shell in the fleeing waves. And the Haustorium, deep in the hold of Nowhere, sloughs from the mountains into shadow. Monsters prowl its darkened ways to eat at me as the surf eats the shore. When I am gone, I might know the bottom of their depthless hearts. When I am gone, I might know those sunless days unburdened by thought beneath the sea. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

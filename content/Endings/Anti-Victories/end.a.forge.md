# Anti-Victory: THE WHITE PENITENCE
In the orders of the Mansus, the Hours reign supreme, who build its walls with stones of Law. Every stone can be broken, but not easily, and the Forge teaches us: the artisan may achieve their highest goal only by destroying their most precious tool. So I am shattered, as once the Flint was; in my place is a cold absence. It is that absence which creeps into the cracks of the walls of Law, which pushes out from within, until all the walls crumble - all but the first Wall of White. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper into darkness. Please accept our congratulations.]<br>**Tarot:** Five of Pentacles

# Anti-Victory: A FINAL DEPARTURE
Death and Eternity are the directions of this world, the axis which shackles Nowhere beneath the earth. For Nowhere to move beyond, it must step outside of both - and I am the stepping stone. Their guiding star strains against the shell of my soul, and one day, it shall break free. Will I die? Will I be born? I only know these shards of truth - it shall be gentle; it shall be cruel; it shall be something new. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

# Anti-Victory: WHAT BREAKS IN THE NIGHT
The war begins, as most wars do, with a single life taken; and for this war, it is mine. Within my empty spaces comes the drowning night, the crushing dissolution, and I am put down as a leak is stopped. I was the first, but I was not the last. Across the world, the Nowhere ingress continues, fought with black water and star-iron teeth. And with hollowed eyes, I shall see it to the end. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

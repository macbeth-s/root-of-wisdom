# Anti-Victory: FROM THE NEST
The serpents of the world have lain in waiting for so long, left to sleep in darkness and weep in storms. But no longer. The Roots stir to life, the Lightning walks once more, and those which nest in me come forth - for Blood too is a serpent. My limbs and my head are not enough to contain them, so they split one into three. From my new mouths spill the praises of their Father, the fanged odes to their Mother, all the waking songs of dawn. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

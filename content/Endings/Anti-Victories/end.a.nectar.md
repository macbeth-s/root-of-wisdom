# Anti-Victory: IN MEMORY YET GREEN
When the Wake was closer to the Wood, our ancestors knew how to make a nameless History from its muck. We have forgotten it, but the nerve-canopy within me still thrums with its words. Now I am surrendered to the earth, where the roots and hyphae join. Bit by bit, they dissemble me, reconfigure me to ride the air. In breath and heart, the new lesson takes root: as within, so without; as without, so within. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

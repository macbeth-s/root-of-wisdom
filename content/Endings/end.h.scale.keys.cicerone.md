## There will be Seven Histories
How many mountains have passed from the world? We do not remember, but the Lost Histories do, and when they are released, as will the dreams of old. In walking those dreams, their mysteries will become apparent to all.

# The Earth's Heart
The Hours have forbidden the opening of the Spider’s Door, but nothing can resist the kiss of the Key of Keys. Fanged with amethyst and black nephrite, it unlocks its every valve and dilates each sphincter; the Histories consumed by Nowhere climb out from its gullet. Tangled in their threads are memories of the mountains raised in the world’s dawn. I will walk these memories, guiding all who remember to the oldest secrets which lie deep in the earth’s heart. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.]

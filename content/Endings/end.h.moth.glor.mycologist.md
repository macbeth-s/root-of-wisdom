## Safety in the Light
In the Glory, we may find our truth and our end. When Nowhere is touched by its Light, it will move beyond life and death, and we will know how to follow. 

# Persistance
Eternity’s light has long opposed the darkness of death. One is destined to consume the other, but to be consumed is not to be destroyed. As light penetrates Nowhere and the nights grow bright, beginnings and ends are bleached into indistinction, the realms of the real and unreal pressing until they rupture into each other. In these herniated spaces, will my studies flourish or falter? Whether one or the other, both or neither come to pass, I can be assured that they will persist evermore. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

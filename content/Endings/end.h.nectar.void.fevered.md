## The Recourse of Life
<br>Those who take their own lives may jump first from the peak of the Temple of the Wheel. Now I jump from the peak of the Threshold - not to take my life, but to give it. Afterwards I join those who went before me into the dark, but here, it is warm and quiet. And when those of the future come to drink our red wisdom, we shall share ourselves with delight. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

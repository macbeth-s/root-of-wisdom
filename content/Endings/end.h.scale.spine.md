## Bed of Bones
<br>At the earth’s highest scars and the sea’s deepest wounds, the bones of stone burst forth to walk the waking world. The uprooted Histories creep into their tombs like ivy, and the beds of the dead turn to terraces. And when they have rested enough, our old souls will stir once more. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.] <br>**Tarot:** Aeon

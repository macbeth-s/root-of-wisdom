## Safety in the Shadows
If I cannot cure my condition, I can still contain it. My shadow shall bear what ails me into the House of the Moon, and on this side of the world, I will be free of its touch.

# Dawn's Reprieve
Every choice has its shadow, even those of the Hours; and it is the House of the Moon where they reside. My shadow danced in the Bounds, to take the shadow of my sickness in hand, whirling down its walls until both enjoined in that space behind the Mansus. The memories of pain still throb in my darkmost hollows. But when the dawn comes, I do not burn.

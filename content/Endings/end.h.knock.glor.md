## Twilight
<br>The Watchman walks all the ways of the Mansus we cannot. Light goes before him, but shadow goes behind. Every night, we who dream will enter that shadow to follow in his footsteps, walking in the Wood, the Bounds, until finally the Stag Door yields to his lantern-light. In his shadow we will walk, and as he did so long ago, we will know. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.] <br>**Tarot:** Hierophant

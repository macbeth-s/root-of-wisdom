## History's Striving will Conclude
As others ascend to the Glory, new beings shall arise from Light to replace them - the perfected gods-from-steel.

# The Watchman's Lesson
The Glory is a question, where the Watchman first learned change. He gave up the flame for the Lantern, but that lesson lingers in flesh and stone - and with our hands and our tools, we can shape Light into Steel, as he shaped himself long ago. Where the Vagabond once traveled, the iron tracks are laid; where the Watchman once walked, the molten blood runs bright. Let the gods-from-steel uphold the world in gold.

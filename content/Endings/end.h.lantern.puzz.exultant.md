## The Watchman's Lesson
<br>The Glory is a question, where the Watchman first learned change. He gave up the flame for the Lantern, but that lesson lingers in flesh and stone - and with our hands and our tools, we can shape Light into Steel, as he shaped himself long ago. Where the Vagabond once traveled, the iron tracks are laid; where the Watchman once walked, the molten blood runs bright. Let the gods-from-steel uphold the world in gold. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper.

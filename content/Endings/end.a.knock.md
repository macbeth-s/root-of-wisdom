# Anti-Victory: BEHIND AND BEYOND
It is said each of us visits a House behind the world at least twice; it was never said to be the same House. So in our final dreams, the gates to the House behind the wave open like a mouth. I am drawn in like flotsam in the tide, like a snowflake in the avalanche. The Stag Door which lies here is not the same shattered one we know, but it is the Stag Door nonetheless, and beyond the ambered gate of golden horn, I - all of us - can truly Know. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

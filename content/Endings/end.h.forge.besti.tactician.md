<br>All weapons of war derive from the Law. If I can reshape it by fang and talon - if I rewrite it in blood and fire - then I can ensure success for every battle to come.\[You've wrought a History which at least one of the Hours will endorse. ## The Trappings of Time
<br>The Law of the Hours clothes them in Eternity’s fabric, but every garment can be torn. The claws of the past shear the cloak of the present. Ancient fangs rip apart the veil of the future. Through these tears, I glimpse the long-finished wars and the War to come. I understand how they were lost, how they were fought; and this most of all: how they will be won. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

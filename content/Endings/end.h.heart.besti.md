## The Law will be Preserved
This History is the culmination of my labors, the fruit of my sacrifice. The Law will be protected by those beyond its power, fangs and talons wielded in service of the world’s persistence.

# Vigilance
The wounding of the Law laid by the gods-from-stone was the first wrong done to the world. All its victims, once expelled from day, now ensure no more may be done. Whatever the Hours raise against each other, whatever they devise against the inconvenient burdens of Law, will fall victim to the monsters they created. And the world carries on.

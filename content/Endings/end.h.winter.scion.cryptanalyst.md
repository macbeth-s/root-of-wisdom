## The Histories Will Unravel
The riddles I received came from someone who is not. It is impossible for them to be answered, but when the Scion arrives, my Interlocutor will teach me to find a world where those answers exist.

# Light in Darkness
The Chandler - who is he? A shadow, a monster - destruction itself? I cannot say, but I can glimpse his shape in every question I received… and I know it was not he who asked them. Ever since the First End, my Interlocutor anticipated the Last. Every riddle warned of this Fate, but each taught how it may be avoided. The End will pass over the dark hollow beneath. And when I return above, the new world will be at its brightest. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

## Nowhere will be Somewhere
The denizens of Nowhere will kindly carry the memories of everything lost beyond our sight. But we will have no need for them. All we will ever need and know is the constancy of steel.

# A Night of Steel
When the world came to know of Nowhere, Nowhere came to know the world. Through that knowledge did the gods-from-Nowhere learn to creep into reality, but as we begin to forget them, their forms return to the darkened sea from which they arose. Their absence is now the mold in which the gods-from-steel are cast, rising from the roiling seas of flame. And our nights are made all the brighter.

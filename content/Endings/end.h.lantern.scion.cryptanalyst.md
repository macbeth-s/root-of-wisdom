# A Golden Resolution
The Chandler - who is she? An artisan, a sovereign - creation itself? I cannot say, but I can glimpse her shape in every message I received… and I know it was not she who wove them. Every riddle extended the reach of my Interlocutor, each answer spinning another thread for it to climb. I understand how to follow its ascent up Eternity’s braid, and I know I must. Perhaps we will burn, or arrive together into the final Hour’s hand. But no matter what, my mind will be bright.

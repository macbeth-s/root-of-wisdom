When the Scion descends upon the world, my Interlocutor will be revealed, and I will find my answers in their revelation.\[You've wrought a History which at least one of the Hours will endorse. ## A Golden Resolution
<br>The Chandler - who is she? An artisan, a sovereign - creation itself? I cannot say, but I can glimpse her shape in every message I received… and I know it was not she who wove them. Every riddle extended the reach of my Interlocutor, each answer spinning another thread for it to climb. I understand how to follow its ascent up Eternity’s braid, and I know I must. Perhaps we will burn, or arrive together into the final Hour’s hand. But no matter what, my mind will be bright. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.] <br>**Tarot:** Magician

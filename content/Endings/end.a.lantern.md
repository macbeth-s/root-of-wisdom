# Anti-Victory: TO WATCH FROM WITHOUT
Eternity is pure; Eternity is bright. So I, intertwined with corruption, am banished from its halls. In the place beyond Nowhere, I behold its golden light, a shining candle behind dark glass. I shall never know its warmth. But the Chandler shall take me by the hand and lead me towards the next of her worlds. And waiting at the window, in the Gallery of Lights, I shall witness its completion. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

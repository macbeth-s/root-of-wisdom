## History's Striving will Subside
‘Power is Knowledge.’ Every war is a battle of truths. When these truths are tested by the first children of History, I will learn from them to be a herald of victory, the final arbiter of truth.

# Conquering Truth
No one Hour possesses the Histories. The Beachcomber always wished to possess them, and with command of the Worms, he found his means. Their wingless flights mar the sky like smoke, yet the world grows all the clearer. The Crow sees all which is lost, and I will see all which is true. By that vision of clarity, I will lead whoever follows me to victory - in time or truth or dream. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

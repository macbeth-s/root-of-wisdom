## The Law will be Transgressed
This History is the culmination of my labors, the fruit of my sacrifice. The Doors will not stop humanity’s passage into the Mansus, and our encroachment into its highest chambers will be exonerated.

# The First and the Last
Once more, the Stag Door shatters, and once more Ghirbi weeps. But here he weeps with joy, as the swarm of humanity wrests him from his post and through the gate. Now we celebrate the entrance of the first Know; and soon, very soon, we will celebrate the entrance of the last.

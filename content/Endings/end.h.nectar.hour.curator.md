## Origination
<br>Every passage leaves its mark. When the gods-from-stone raised the Mansus and passed from the unknown into reality, those marks remained. Now the roots of this new history travel along them like blood through veins. Their source lies in the Haustorium, and when they reach the long-lost world, its new-old secrets shall flourish across every stone. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

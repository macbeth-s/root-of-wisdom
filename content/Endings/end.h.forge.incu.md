# The Endless Dawn
When the Cuckoo’s Egg burns in the Malleary, its shell sheds cinders onto the Mansus-winds, drawing the Dead to seek the ashen Years within. Those who reach out are rebirthed in warm flesh, their lost voices reclaimed, their life restored. Now we need no Hours to live Long- not when each death can bring forth a new beginning.

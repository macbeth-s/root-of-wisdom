## History's Striving will Conclude
<br>This History is the culmination of labors, the fruit of my sacrifice. The heir will come from the Glory and grace the Histories with their presence, and they shall inherit Eternity for us.\[You've wrought a History which at least one of the Hours will endorse. This is the point of no return. Many of these Histories are strange or wild. Unless you're certain you want to end the game, remain rooted here in the Haustorium.]
## The Golden Child
<br>When she started her descent from Glory, the world watched her approach like a sunset or a storm. Candles burn brighter while all other lights dim; so we roll our wax and braid our wicks, raise them high to be the first one holding Eternity’s Flame. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

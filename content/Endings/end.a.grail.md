# Anti-Victory: A SHARED BIRTH
The child of two immortals is a crime and a criminal, whose creators are punished with its consumption. What is devoured cannot be undevoured, yet neither is it unborn. In Nowhere, they are eternal children kept in the care of the Applebright, seeds in her flesh. If planted, they may grow once more; but all seeds need soil, and that is what I become. When they are reborn outside the Laws of Upper Heaven, I shall live on inside them; and how might the world change then? \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

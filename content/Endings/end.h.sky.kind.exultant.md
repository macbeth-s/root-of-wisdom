## History's Striving will Subside
<br>It will be kinder for the world to forget its most painful pasts. In their place we will remember the gods-from-Steel that have always cared, always comforted, and always been.\[You've wrought a History which at least one of the Hours will endorse. This is the point of no return. Many of these Histories are strange or wild. Unless you're certain you want to end the game, remain rooted here in the Haustorium.]
## Perfect Design
<br>When the ever-bleeding Sun departs, the mouths of glass drink his blood. From the Stone which was always shattered, the fingers of steel pick out their feast. The gods-from-steel never faltered in their light or warmth or shape, and so they stand above every Hour, the eternal beacons for which the world was made. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.] <br>**Tarot:** Picota

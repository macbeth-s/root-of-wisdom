## A New History will be Rooted
With the monstrous gods below and the callous gods above, we must search in the Bounds between. There, the new Threshold will arise, where we mortals can find a true sanctuary.

# The Recourse of Thorns
As once the gods-from-stone raised the Mansus from the Wood, we raise a new House rooted deep in the Bounds. Its Walls are flushed skin and its Doors are breath’s passage; the Wood around us is tressed with hair and barbed with bones. In its safe heart, we may whisper of the perils outside; whisper of the day their danger may be not remembered, but imagined.

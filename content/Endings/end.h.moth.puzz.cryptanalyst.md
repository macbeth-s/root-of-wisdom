## Safety in the Light
<br>My Interlocutor whispered riddles of light and darkness, absence and substance. When Nowhere enters the Glory, I will have my answer; and I will receive one in return.\[You've wrought a History which at least one of the Hours will endorse. This is the point of no return. Many of these Histories are strange or wild. Unless you're certain you want to end the game, remain rooted here in the Haustorium.]
## Moonlit Whispers
<br>‘What is not seen?’ The answer is heard in the winds of Nowhere - entangled in black branches, bending the trees to speak. From there did my Interlocutor offer up riddles, each different in name, each with the same answer: Nowhere. On that answer did she climb up out of my mouth and into the Light. But left beneath my tongue is my reward of why: the sought is sweeter than the given. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.] <br>**Tarot:** High Priestess

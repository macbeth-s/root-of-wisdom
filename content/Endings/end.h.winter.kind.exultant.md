## The Histories Will Unravel
<br>The threads of History are tainted with cruelty, so they must be ended and fall to Nowhere. But I will leave behind one memento by which the world afterward can begin- woven together by hands of steel.\[You've wrought a History which at least one of the Hours will endorse. This is the point of no return. Many of these Histories are strange or wild. Unless you're certain you want to end the game, remain rooted here in the Haustorium.]
## Slumber Sublime
<br>The unraveling is gentle. Night comes, we sleep, and we neither dream nor wake. All of us are Nowhere, the threads of History curled around us like a spool or a coil. As sleep softly leaves me, I watch the new Names loom above, pirn-spined and rapiered, picking out the threads for the world to come. Let tomorrow gleam bright. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.] <br>**Tarot:** Hanged Man

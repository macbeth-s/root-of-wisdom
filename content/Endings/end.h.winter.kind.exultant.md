## The Histories Will Unravel
The threads of History are tainted with cruelty, so they must be ended and fall to Nowhere. But I will leave behind one memento by which the world afterward can begin- woven together by hands of steel.

# Slumber Sublime
The unraveling is gentle. Night comes, we sleep, and we neither dream nor wake. All of us are Nowhere, the threads of History curled around us like a spool or a coil. As sleep softly leaves me, I watch the new Names loom above, pirn-spined and rapiered, picking out the threads for the world to come. Let tomorrow gleam bright. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper.

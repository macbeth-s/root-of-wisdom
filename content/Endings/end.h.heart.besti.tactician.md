## War's Culmination
<br>The wounding of the Law laid by the gods-from-stone was the first wrong done to the world. All its victims, once expelled from day, now ensure no more may be done. Whatever the Hours raise against each other, whatever they devise against the inconvenient burdens of Law, will fall victim to the monsters they created. All these rules written in blood are revealed like blossoms. And at their sanguine conjunction, my unyielding strategy will come to fruition. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

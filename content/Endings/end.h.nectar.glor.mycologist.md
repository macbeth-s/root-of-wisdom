## A New History will be Rooted
In darkness, all shapes lose their form. In that dark and fertile soil where a new History is planted, I will find the murky space outside life and death, and let it persist in this arising past.

# Shared Warmths
The Crowned Growth and the Sun-in-Rags have been long opposed, fire against spores and ice against comforts. But there can be no light without darkness, nor darkness without light, and shadows stretch longest at sunset. Where darkness meets light, the lines between life and death blur; and in that crimson haze, this new History will reweave them into something far greater.

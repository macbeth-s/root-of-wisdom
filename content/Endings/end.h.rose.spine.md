<br>The earth is the deepest road, and when the mountains stretch up off the ground and the clouds slough off their peaks like a blanket, that road will pass through the yawning skies. The pilgrims of Nowhere shall gratefully walk it to Somewhere beyond the Mansus, the Hours, the World. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

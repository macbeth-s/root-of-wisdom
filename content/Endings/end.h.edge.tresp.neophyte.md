## The Necessary Conquest
<br>Nowhere lies behind a scar of black nephrite, a border inviolate since the Mansus was first raised. It is the refuge untouched by the strivings of the real Hours. But now the Colonel takes the first necessary step as he once did upon the sands. In darkness, in silence, in emptiness, he will lead existence to victory. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

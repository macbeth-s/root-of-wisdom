## Safety through Strength
The real Hours cannot cross the threshold to Nowhere, but its inhabitants can, safe in its refuge. An Hour of cunning will violate that boundary first to keep the enemies of existence from ever crossing it again.

# The Necessary Conquest
Nowhere lies behind a scar of black nephrite, a border inviolate since the Mansus was first raised. It is the refuge untouched by the strivings of the real Hours. But now the Colonel takes the first necessary step as he once did upon the sands. In darkness, in silence, in emptiness, he will lead existence to victory. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.]

# Anti-Victory: OUR GLORIOUS GUIDE
It is impossible for the Glory to enter Nowhere - unless one pays the proper price. Now it is paid with my dissolution. For a moment, I become a viscid window, and in that moment, Light passes to illuminate the empty truth of Nowhere. The Hours below are gone; but the Mare-in-the-Tree knew Light first, and from Light is she reborn. She shall lead us by the hand to the Glory, blinded by her mercy, until we may understand at last, at last, at last - \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

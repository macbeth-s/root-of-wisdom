# Dreams of Dawn
The Hours have forbidden the opening of the Spider’s Door, but nothing can resist the kiss of the Key of Keys. Fanged with amethyst and black nephrite, it unlocks its every valve and dilates each sphincter; the Histories consumed by Nowhere climb out from its gullet. They will visit our dreams as the bee with the flower, until our oldest pasts finally emerge. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

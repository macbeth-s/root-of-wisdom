## The Histories Will Unravel
This History is the culmination of my labors, the fruit of my sacrifice. The world will forget the cruel knowledge of existence and fall to Nowhere, in the kindness of ignorance.

# Softest Slumber
The unraveling is gentle. Night comes, we sleep, and we neither dream nor wake. All of us are Nowhere, the remnants of History strewn around us like a nest or a caul. And soon, sleep softly leaves us as we forget our births. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

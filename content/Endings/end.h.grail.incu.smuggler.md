## The Law will be Defied
What I have brought is not safe in the Hours’ sight. So I shall set it free beneath the branches of an Hour on the other side of the Law. It will consume me, but once it does, we will be together; we will be free.

# A Succulent Land
The Seglaz swam the shining sea until they were driven into its depths. For so long, my companion languished in hunger, fed only by what fell into darkness. Now I hold her mouths to the dripping branches of the Malachite, let her suckle on red-gold honey, until she turns her mouths upon me. I am consumed, but I am not dead. I am with her in the only way which matters, and as we swim through the earth, the world has never tasted sweeter.

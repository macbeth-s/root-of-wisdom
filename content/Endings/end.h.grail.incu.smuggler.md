## A Succulent Land
<br>The Seglaz swam the shining sea until they were driven into its depths. For so long, my companion languished in hunger, fed only by what fell into darkness. Now I hold her mouths to the dripping branches of the Malachite, let her suckle on red-gold honey, until she turns her mouths upon me. I am consumed, but I am not dead. I am with her in the only way which matters, and as we swim through the earth, the world has never tasted sweeter. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

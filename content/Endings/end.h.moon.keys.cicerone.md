## Safety in the Shadows
Every mountain in our world is but a reflection of the Mansus. So when the House of the Moon, the Mansus-shadow, opens in its entirety, we will come to know its ways.

# The Journey of Night
In the shadow of the Forge, the Flint found her end. In the shadow of the Mansus, Nowhere finds its final home. In fire and light were the paths of the Mansus carved, and by night-jewel and worm-stone, the Key of Keys carves open the paths into the House of the Moon. We in the waking world cannot walk them; but our shadow-reflections, our mirror-twins will. And in the space between dreams, these secrets shall enter us like rain upon the sea.

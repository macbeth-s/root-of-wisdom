## An Unknown Night
<br>‘What is not seen?’ The Pilgrim visited Nowhere, in her youth, and swore to never return when she first arose with the Sun. But the gods-from-Nowhere she spoke with still stand in her shadow unseen, in the darkness behind her. Will she rise above the Glory? Will she falter in its Light? Wherever she goes, Nowhere will follow; and the world they leave behind will know them no more. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

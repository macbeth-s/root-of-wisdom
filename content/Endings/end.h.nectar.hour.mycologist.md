## A Foliage of Frost
<br>The Histories are the gardens of the Hours, where the Dead are naught but mulch and mortar. When the seeds of our history take root in distant lands, death will no longer draw us down, but beyond. And past the walls of starlight, those who are Dead - those who shall become so - may find life once more in this living History. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

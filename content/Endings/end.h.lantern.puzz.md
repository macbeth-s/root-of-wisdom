## The Watchman's Answer
<br>The Glory is a question. We know not the Watchman’s answer, but his answer was always First. The Vagabond echoed his words, which her fellow Pilgrims followed to the place above the House. They went beyond our sight, but their shadows have grown bright gold. Take a polished piece in hand; let the warm weight of Eternity come upon you. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

<br>What I have longs for freedom in Light, but the sight of the Hours will destroy it. But in the Cuckoo’s Egg, it can dwell in light concealed in shadow, to hatch into its destined form… and to elevate my destiny beside it.\[You've wrought a History which at least one of the Hours will endorse. ## Born in Light
<br>The Unwise Mortal followed his master into the Glory, where he found his greatest form. Now I follow my ward into Nowhere, the darkness lit by old fires, and there witness the glorious revival of the Scīmafectra, as once we witnessed their descent from the Sun. On its scales will I find the dreams that were, the dreams that will be… and learn to make my own dreams the beacon all will follow. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.] <br>**Tarot:** High Priestess

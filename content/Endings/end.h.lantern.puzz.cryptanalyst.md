## History's Striving will Conclude
I am not the first to whom my Interlocutor spoke, but I may be the first to understand. In this History, guided by lost light, I will answer their desperate message.

# The Watchman's Mercy
The Glory is a question, and to answer it is loss. The Watchman knows not what he surrendered, but in the eons since his ascension, he has asked for its return. In the glassy rivers and sun-crested clouds, I glimpsed the shards of shattered mercy. I will hold it in my hand like a mirror, and when the Watchman looks upon me, he and I shall once more know his oldest light.

## The Watchman's Mercy
<br>The Glory is a question, and to answer it is loss. The Watchman knows not what he surrendered, but in the eons since his ascension, he has asked for its return. In the glassy rivers and sun-crested clouds, I glimpsed the shards of shattered mercy. I will hold it in my hand like a mirror, and when the Watchman looks upon me, he and I shall once more know his oldest light. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

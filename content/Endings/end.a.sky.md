# Anti-Victory: THE LAST THE FIRST
The Histories strive towards Light, and it is the Glory which provides; yet the Glory also burns. As they reach ever-higher, they now tread too close. So many become the smokeless fuel for the purest flame, all but one which remained apart. Did I live in that History? My skin is foamed light and my flesh a starry haze; but in that History my bones remain buried, and in their dark facets, my truest self remains. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

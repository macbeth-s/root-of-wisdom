## The Law will be Transgressed
<br>No one is safe from forbidden things outside the fortress of the Mansus. Humanity must pass its Doors to find true safety, for our only sanctuary free from the world’s dangers lies in Dream.\[You've wrought a History which at least one of the Hours will endorse. This is the point of no return. Many of these Histories are strange or wild. Unless you're certain you want to end the game, remain rooted here in the Haustorium.]
## Sanctum of Dream
<br>Once we are all accounted Know, the Mare burns out forever. When our souls withdraw into Dream, the Growth loses its grasp for good. Humanity will reside like rats in the Mansus, but though we are disdained, we are tolerated. Perhaps the freedom of History is lost, but to be free from Nowhere is a far more precious gift. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.] <br>**Tarot:** Hierophant

## The Law will be Transgressed
No one is safe from forbidden things outside the fortress of the Mansus. Humanity must pass its Doors to find true safety, for our only sanctuary free from the world’s dangers lies in Dream.

# Sanctum of Dream
Once we are all accounted Know, the Mare burns out forever. When our souls withdraw into Dream, the Growth loses its grasp for good. Humanity will reside like rats in the Mansus, but though we are disdained, we are tolerated. Perhaps the freedom of History is lost, but to be free from Nowhere is a far more precious gift. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.]

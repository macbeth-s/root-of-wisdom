## Nowhere will be Somewhere
<br>This History is the culmination of my labors, the fruit of my sacrifice. The gods-from-Nowhere will travel to the origin of Stone, where they will be beyond us - and truly be.\[You've wrought a History which at least one of the Hours will endorse. This is the point of no return. Many of these Histories are strange or wild. Unless you're certain you want to end the game, remain rooted here in the Haustorium.]
## True Somewhere
<br>The arbiters of day condemn the Mare-in-the-Tree as a deception, a false Hour. When she departs for the home of Stone, beyond the reach of the Sun, Nowhere rides her red flames until they wink out of the night sky. Perhaps somewhere, her sweet lies ring with truth, but this world is made all the wiser without her temptations. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.] <br>**Tarot:** Bateleur

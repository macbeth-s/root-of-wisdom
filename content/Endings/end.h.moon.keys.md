## Safety in the Shadows
<br>This History is the culmination of my labors, the fruit of my sacrifice. The Key will throw open the House of the Moon to receive the scourges of Nowhere, locking away their danger from the world.\[You've wrought a History which at least one of the Hours will endorse. ## The Softest Night
<br>In a house of black sapphire and ivory, of scolecite and flint, the Key of Keys opens all the Doors through which Nowhere passes, to join with the Summit Gate, entrance to the House of the Moon. The world suffers the scourge of Nowhere no more, free from the nightmares which pierce the world’s skin. Only our own shadow remains, our trailing reflection, the waves of night breaking on the shores of day. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.] <br>**Tarot:** Justice

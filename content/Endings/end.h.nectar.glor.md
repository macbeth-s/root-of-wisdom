## A New History will be Rooted
<br>This History is the culmination of my labors, the fruit of my sacrifice. The Histories grow in shadow, and in the deepest shadows of all, the seeds of a new History will grow.\[You've wrought a History which at least one of the Hours will endorse. This is the point of no return. Many of these Histories are strange or wild. Unless you're certain you want to end the game, remain rooted here in the Haustorium.]
## Symbiosis
<br>The First History was written in the twilight of the gods-from-stone, in the stretching shadows of their deaths at the world’s dawn. Beneath the pale noon, no quarter for shadow remains in the world. But there is always darkness below. In these caverns, the seeds for the Next History are sown and scrawled upon their walls. When the time is right, they will bloom beneath the Sun. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.] <br>**Tarot:** Eclaircissement

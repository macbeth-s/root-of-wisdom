## The Law will be Remade
This History is the culmination of my labors, the fruit of my sacrifice. The Law has wronged so many, so the fangs and talons of its victims shall carve out due recompense from their tormentors.

# Our Time
The Law of the Hours denied so many their rightful time beneath the Sun. It is only right for this wrong to be amended. Teeth and talons tear at the Hours, the lost Years flowing from their wounds. They will become the wellspring of life which all can drink from, mortals made immortal, forever unbound by the Long chains of the Law.

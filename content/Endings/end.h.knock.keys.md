## An Exposition in Dream
<br>The Mansus is barred to those mortals who are not Know. It is the Calyptra which denies, and the Chancel which limits, but even they cannot defy the Key of Keys. As its teeth sink into the Stag Door, its scarred surfaces grind open, and the way to knowledge is forever exposed. Some will gladly pass; others may resist its pull; but in the end, every one of us will stand before the horns, we will pass beneath them - we will come to Know.

## The Law will be Defied
This History is the culmination of my labors, the fruit of my sacrifice. The Hours will follow the trail of Stone to a world born before the Law, where they can fulfill their most forbidden desires.

# A Plentiful Harvest
To commit the Crime of the Sky is to be set outside the Law, and so no Hour - no living Hour - has dared risk its commission. But in the worlds beyond the reach of the Law etched in stone, the Hours will find an opportunity. Not all will indulge, nor will all return, but as the stars part and the earth overflows, I know those who do shall share their most nourishing fruits. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.]

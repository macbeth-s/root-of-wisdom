# A Poison Tree
The Malachite renews the Cuckoo’s Egg with her honey, seeping in like water through stone. It is brought to life, and with it, new appetites. We hear neither the crack nor the feast, but we hear the slithering of hungry roots beneath and the buzzing of frenzied bees in our blood, all seeking the sweetness left in the world’s unopened veins.

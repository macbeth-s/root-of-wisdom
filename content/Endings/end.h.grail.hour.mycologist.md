## Return in Resplendence
<br>The alukites have no place in the House, save for the hollow beneath the Ascent of Knives. It is they who first feel their long-dead hearts beat again, who sense the presence of those bloodied, corrupted Hours. And when they step into the Mansus-Light, they are not consumed; their teeth shine with the red resplendence of life. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

## There will be Seven Histories
The world need not travel to the mountains to know them when the mountains rise to meet it. But all travelers need a guide through unknown ways, so I shall guide them to spread their secrets.

# Tour of Bones
Ambered limbs and reefed legs reach down from the peaks and step onto the shores. Each step is an earthquake, each breath an avalanche, and their rumblings stir the Children of the Earth to join in the songs of lost Histories. We will step upon their heads and walk their winding courses until we finally reach their knotted heart. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

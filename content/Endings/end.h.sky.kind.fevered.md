## Peaceful Concession
<br>Was it my sickness which pained me, or the memories of life before it? Each bears their weight, but as this amnesia buries me, my many pasts alight as the birds depart in winter. Here is the curing touch not of Law, but of Freedom: from all I ever was and all I might have been. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

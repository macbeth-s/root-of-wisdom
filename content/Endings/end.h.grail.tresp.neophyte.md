## The Law will be Defied
The forbidden things which creep into the world cannot be touched outside the Law. So a ceaseless Hour will commit a forgiven crime to become a monstrous guardian, protecting us from the monsters beyond.

# His Crimson Vigil
The Thunderskin is scaled, his skin adorned with mouths. His dance demands blood, so we spill it freely beneath his endless storm. We fear his roiling clouds and sanguine rain, but we fear those beyond him much more. In time, we will learn to love him - he is not the first protector requiring our blood, after all. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

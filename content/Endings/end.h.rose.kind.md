## Nowhere will be Somewhere
<br>This History is the culmination of my labors, the fruit of my sacrifice. The world will forget Nowhere, or Nowhere forget the world; and so Nowhere will move on to a world beyond us.\[You've wrought a History which at least one of the Hours will endorse. ## A Night of Peace
<br>When the world came to know of Nowhere, Nowhere came to know the world. Through that knowledge did the gods-from-Nowhere learn to creep into reality, but as we begin to forget them, their forms return to the darkened sea from which they arose. They will not stay there forever, but they will not storm our world’s shores again. And our nights once more are peace. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.] <br>**Tarot:** Bateleur

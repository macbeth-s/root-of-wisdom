## Nowhere will be Somewhere
This History is the culmination of my labors, the fruit of my sacrifice. The world will forget Nowhere, or Nowhere forget the world; and so Nowhere will move on to a world beyond us.

# A Night of Peace
When the world came to know of Nowhere, Nowhere came to know the world. Through that knowledge did the gods-from-Nowhere learn to creep into reality, but as we begin to forget them, their forms return to the darkened sea from which they arose. They will not stay there forever, but they will not storm our world’s shores again. And our nights once more are peace.

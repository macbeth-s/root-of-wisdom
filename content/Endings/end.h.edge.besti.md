# Bed of Beasts
Many monsters dwell in Nowhere, yet many more live in the world. All those who find no place in the day now find their purpose in the night. The evenings fill with their sounds - gnawing at the door, wings curtaining the window; but the monsters we know do not trouble our dreams. In their clawed grasp, we are safe.

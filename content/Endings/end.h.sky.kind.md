## History's Striving will Subside
This History is the culmination of my labors, the fruit of my sacrifice. The world will forget all but one of its pasts and find the peace and kindness of Eternity in what remains.

# Pale Liberation
The contradictions of History are a net the Hours will not untangle. But just as Alexander did at Gordion, just as the Colonel did to the Seven-Coils, we can cut ourselves free of our past. As we shrug off Time’s fraying threads, let them fall where they may; for in this truest, only History, we may finally drift into Eternity’s embrace. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

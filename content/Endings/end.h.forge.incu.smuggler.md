## Flight of Freedom
<br>For so long the Glaeterfleoge sought the Light, and the Light consumed them, until not even bone remains. But now the sparks of life nest in their cinders, and dust sheds like the ecdysis from their wings of black and white. My companion will fly free on the winds of fortune, and I will follow in her shadow. To her ashes I will attend - and perhaps someday, mine will join hers, to know that pale joy of flight. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

## The Law will be Remade
What I brought will be killed should the Hours know of it. But when one can return to life from death, what reason is there to fear it? It will go free in the Wake and the Mansus, and I will be at its side, a hunter at home.

# Flight of Freedom
For so long the Glaeterfleoge sought the Light, and the Light consumed them, until not even bone remains. But now the sparks of life nest in their cinders, and dust sheds like the ecdysis from their wings of black and white. My companion will fly free on the winds of fortune, and I will follow in her shadow. To her ashes I will attend - and perhaps someday, mine will join hers, to know that pale joy of flight.

## Safety in the Light
<br>This History is the culmination of my labors, the fruit of my sacrifice. The gods-from-Nowhere are shadows cast by the Glory. When once its Light scorches them, we will forever be safe from its touch.\[You've wrought a History which at least one of the Hours will endorse. This is the point of no return. Many of these Histories are strange or wild. Unless you're certain you want to end the game, remain rooted here in the Haustorium.]
## Light's Vigilance
<br>Light always comes before shadow. Where the gods-from-Nowhere intends to arise, the burning aureoles of the gods-from-light have arrived to strike them down. The nights dazzle with their dance of light and darkness. Here once more is the shelter cast by the first fires, constant as the stars. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game.

## The Histories Will Unravel
<br>The consumers of History will someday unravel them. Their hunger cannot be denied, but it can be followed. I will lead the warmakers to the last corner to be consumed, where we might be the first and final victors.\[You've wrought a History which at least one of the Hours will endorse. This is the point of no return. Many of these Histories are strange or wild. Unless you're certain you want to end the game, remain rooted here in the Haustorium.]
## Crown of Victories
<br>The victor of every war is not the one who has slain, but the one who remains. Beneath the auspices of broken glass, following the trails of the god-eaters, I learn the ways one remains, even one all else falls around us. There will be nary a world for the Sun-in-Rags to rule, nary one for the Worms to inhabit; but the world will be nonetheless, and I will lead its final victors. \[You have shaped the Histories; set a stone in their foundation; reached a destination; finished the game. The roots of the world run ever deeper. Please accept our congratulations.] <br>**Tarot:** Hanged Man

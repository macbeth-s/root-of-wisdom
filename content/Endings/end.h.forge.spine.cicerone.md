# An Open Foundation
The Mansus; the World-Spine; the House of the Sun: the fortress of dreams is all of these, but first and foremost, it is a mountain. As the bones burst through its walls, they release the secrets of their stones: the stolen speech of the Dead, the forgotten shapes of its Ways, the bright dust buried beneath the weight of Time. In this land of broken Law, my hands will lay down the trails to lead the world up towards our future, and down our many pasts.

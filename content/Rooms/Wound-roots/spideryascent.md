# Spidery Ascent  
The histories, says the Rising Spider, are not braided like hair nor tangled like roots. They are woven, like webs. Beware, for still she rises.  
## Long Descent, Longer Ascent (Locked)  
Walls sink into crushing darkness while stairs endlessly rise towards a distant light. Such an impossible place can be corrected, but it requires the invocation of the greater authorities of the Upper Heavens.

## Art Description
*A long, creepy natural chute. Three massive tapestries of uneven shape spread up the chute, though the fringes of the tapestries reveal them to be of web and not wool. Towards the bottom, there are some handholds; abandoned spelunking equipment dangles from the angstloch above. At least one dead spelunker is there, mushrooms in their ribcage and spiders crawling out their eye.*

# Escape  
It is between glimpses and reflections of careful lantern-light that Quilaco, then Menard, then Cipagauta (and many a Wayfinder or dragonslayer before them) have seen and spoken to the Feathered Woman. Each time, she has offered them first the choice to jump and be free, then the choice to listen.  
## Primordial Question (Locked)  
Here is a riddle only certain truths can solve. Do I have an answer?  
**Essential:** Numen  
**Required:** 5 Moon, 5 Scale, 5 Nectar  
  
Opening it will spawn [[feathered.woman|The Feathered Woman]].

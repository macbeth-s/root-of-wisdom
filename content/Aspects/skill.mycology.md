# Mycology
A skill that can be used to explore the myriad mysteries of mushrooms. \[Mycology skills can be used at a workstation to craft fungi into powerful materials, or to preserve spores for future use.]<br>
![[skill.mycology.png]]
## Skills
The following skills have the Mycology aspect:
- [[s.transcendence.ascendance|Transcendence & Ascendance]]
- [[s.fermentation.sustentation|Fermentation & Sustentation]]
- Orchids & Narcotics
- Leaves & Thorns
- Putrefactions & Calcinations

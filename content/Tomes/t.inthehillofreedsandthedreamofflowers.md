# In the Hill of Reeds and the Dream of Flowers
The ithast, or ithast-collective, titled the Mariachi of Mirage praise the Prince of Flowers for his gifts, as well as his surreptitious works as the Contentum alongside two other Hours ‘of bone and body.’
## I'm Reading...
The Contentum are praised for raising knowledge from Death, ensuring its persistence, and for the ecstasy in its attainment. ‘By their works do the roots of the Watchman’s Tree run deep and strong. Even uprooted, the Tree remains, although stranger seasons come about thereby…’
## I've Read...
‘He names those nameless desires, forms those formless pleasures, and resolves contradiction into satisfaction.’ The text elaborates how a contradiction of ‘regressive growth’ can be revealed by invoking the Edicts Hospitable before a sovereign seat of the Contentum, when one is accompanied by a ‘crowned coil-rooted limb’.

# Matters of Many Faces
In which the mystic and syncretist Almudena Shuña explores the possibility that the Company Anchorite was dedicated to no Hour, but to the mysterious and ephemeral force known in the western invisible world as Janus.
## I'm Reading...
Shuña speculates the members of the Company Anchorite traversed the world to search for manifestations of Janus, the perhaps avatar or arbiter of the Hours and their Law. Ganesha, in the Land of the River. Ikenga, in the Home of Four Hundred and One. Viracocha, in the Fourfold Realm…
## I've Read...
Shuña does not know why the Company Anchorite would seek Janus, nor is she certain that Janus is truly separate from the Hours, but suggests - furtively, uneasily - that the Company sought to understand the Law through him; and in so doing, learn to transgress its most fundamental rules…

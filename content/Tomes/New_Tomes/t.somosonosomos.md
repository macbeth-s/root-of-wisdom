# Somos O No Somos
Prophecies from the Warlock of the Andes addressed to international - perhaps even intertemporal - sects of the Shorn Brotherhood, called the Four Winds and the Three Flames.
## I'm Reading...
The language used here suggests these prophecies to be orders, though shrouded in layers of allegory. Discerning anything will require time and effort, but it appears to be tied with the Great Inks which can only be used beneath the Watchman’s Tree.
## I've Read...
From their earthen strongholds, the Brotherhood concerned themselves with who composed the Haustorium’s faculty, seeking sympathizers who may use the Great Inks to further their cause across the Histories. The details of these plans are elusive, but I am left to wonder if my safe arrival involved a little more than luck…

# What is Not Known
A discourse on the Secret Histories, and the Hours who possess them, the Vagabond and the Mother of Ants, by a rogue Wayfinder who claims herself ‘an outcast of outcasts’.
## I'm Reading...
‘If the Secret Histories were known, they would not be secret. If the Secret Histories were unknown, they would not be histories. They are strung on the warp of Hours, and we Know walk the weft.’
## I've Read...
‘The Vagabond travels all the Histories, while the Mother travels between. Yet they risk destroying the very Histories they treasure. So they make alliances, lend stories to other tongues, and keep their mouths closed until the proper time.’

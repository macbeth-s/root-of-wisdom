# The Night-Tipped Needle
A manual of tattooing detailing the usage of strange inks to impart tattoos with mystical properties.
## I'm Reading...
Spies of the Shadowless Empire tattooed themselves with the Ink of Containment called catwink to divert attention from themselves. The Unnumbered Legion, the secret army of Rome, later adopted these practices in service to the Chiliarch, said to favor those marked with ‘shadow-scars’.

# What Was Stolen in Splendor
A folk legend on 'the thieving of the Sun' which propagated throughout the Levant in the eighth century. The Church blamed its spread on the remoboths, which led to concerted efforts at suppressing them.
## I'm Reading...
The Laughingthrush and the Sun-in-Splendor regaled to each other the stories of the places they could not go. She spoke of the Root-Ways untouched by his Light. He spoke of the Mansus-Ways her feet had not touched. Together, they conspired to travel where they were forbidden…
## I've Read...
The Laughingthrush stole away the eagle-eyes of the Sun, while the Sun stole away the mortal-masks of the Laughingthrush. The highmost vistas of the Mansus became hers, while the lowborn avatars of the Wake became his. As thieves, they respected the other; as victims, they pitied the other; so they promised to share the many tales they would garner from their spoils, for what was a tale without someone to hear of it?

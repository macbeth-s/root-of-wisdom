# For Want of a Future
An examination of the phenomena colloquially known as pentiments by Socorro Valenzuela.
## I'm Reading...
‘It is the Hours who determine the course of history, yet even they cannot foresee every outcome. When the world diverges in unintended ways, it is quickly sent down a different path, and the other struck from existence. But the maps of these courses remain; as do its seekers, and the tools made to reach it.’
## I've Read...
Valenzuela believes pentiments are a sort of crossroads to other Histories, including those which never came to pass. She believes they are the main medium through which the Worms escape Nowhere, and proposes the pentiments created by the Intercalate allowed them to enter the world in thentofore unseen numbers.

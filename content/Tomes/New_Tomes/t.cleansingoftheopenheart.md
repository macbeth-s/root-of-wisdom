# Cleansing of the Open Heart
Methods on the containment and curtailing of ‘virulent dreams and diseases’ by Father Xavier Saavedra, CA, penned at the beginning of his rectorship, shortly after his predecessor’s disappearance by ‘spindle-fingered spirits’.
## I'm Reading...
Saavedra begins with a declaration of his mission: to identify and root out the spiritual and physical corruption of the Haustorium, and to restore the reputation of the Colegio de Santa Potenciana as a place of learning. He first expels members of the Company Anchorite he finds guilty of ‘corporeal desecration’, but soon moves on to less human targets…
## I've Read...
Saavedra describes undiscovered species of fungi native to the Haustorium, matching their emergence to tragedies in its history. One of them, the New Haustoria Root, is called ‘the courtier and king’ of these numerous species. He identifies the throne at the Colegio’s peak to contain ‘the heart of the Haustoria’, and believes a blow could be dealt against its influence if rites of Abjuration and Limitation were performed before it. However, he is hesitant to acquire the specimen necessary to do so, having witnessed it ‘devour greater hearts’ than his.

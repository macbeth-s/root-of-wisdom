# Resolutions For the Preening of the Continent
Following the expulsion of the Company Anchorite in 1766, a contingent of Obliviates from the Old World came to the Haustorium to devise strategies against the growing numbers of Worms - both the parasitic and political kinds.
## I'm Reading...
The topics fluctuate between reports on the decline of the continental monarchies, the advent of the Worm Wars, and gloating over the Company’s expulsion. A number of the Obliviates express affinities for the Birds of a Feather, and apparently suspected the Company of facilitating Wormish agendas across the New World.
## I've Read...
Their discussions culminate in the creation of a vicious weapon. With the Buzzing in the Brain as bait, the Obliviates can trap a Moth-spirit in corporeal form with their order’s arts, capable of unleashing a ‘tempestuous assault’ to scour the flesh. They are eager to use it against the Worms, but remain concerningly vague as to which kinds…

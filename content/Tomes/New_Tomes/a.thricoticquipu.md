# Thricotic Quipu
A quipu made of human hair recording the history of Quchanina, a Moth-long of a kind called ‘uyanantin’, and of her legacy amongst the Curicuillorians.<br>
Initial Location: [[museumofoldthings|Museum of Old Things]]
## I'm Reading...
When Quchanina arrived at the Haustorium, she was on the cusp of ascension. Beatriz Huaylas offered assistance in her final rites so long as she joined the Curicuillorians as a servant of the Witch-and-Sister. Before attaining immortality, she gave birth to a child, for a uyanantin such as her could not leave the Wood unless someone bearing her blood lived on the other side.
## I've Read...
Quchanina’s descendants contributed hair to this quipu to mark her visits every year. For two centuries, she graced the halls of the Haustorium with Wood-lore and Witch-magics, but they gradually decrease in frequency. Only a single knot marks the last cord, left by Almudena Shuña in 1770, the very year she disappeared. It’s very unlikely Quchanina will ever come here again.

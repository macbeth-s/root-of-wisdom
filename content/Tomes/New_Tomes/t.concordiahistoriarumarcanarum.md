# Concordia Historiarum Arcanarum
Considered the foremost and most censured work of Luis Bahamonde, often translated as ‘The Harmony of Secret Histories’. The Haustorium is sometimes considered to be a corrupted namesake of this work.
## I'm Reading...
Bahamonde attempts to synthesize the contradictions and divergences of the five major Histories, proposing they all stem from a common source. He cites as proof the Intercalate, which left lasting effects across the Histories, even those disregarded by the Sun-in-Splendor.
## I've Read...
Bahamonde is left with numerous lacunae in his unified History, but he enfolds these gaps into his theory, naming these to be wounds left by amputated Histories. Such talk comes close to heresy, but contemporary Inquisitors found themselves far too overwhelmed to do much more than haphazardly limit its propagation.

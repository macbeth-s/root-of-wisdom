# A Night of Serious Dreaming
The self-declared 'bosky anthrographer' Taglufon writes on the procession of dreams through the Wood and into the Wake.
## I'm Reading...
‘The Velvet eats up dreams which crawl across her black belly. The Spider catches dreams which fly through its tangled web. But the Mother of Ants lends her knifley keys to aid her children’s escape.’
## I've Read...
Taglufon describes how visions may travel within mundane memories to escape the attention of ‘Ensnarers in the Tree’. He likens memory to a shell, and indicates that if one is dissolved in ‘the Moth’s Separations’, it will reveal the key to ‘the Knowledge of Night.’

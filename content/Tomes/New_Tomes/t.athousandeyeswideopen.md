# A Thousand Eyes Wide Open
International accounts of a certain ‘dream of eyes’ are dissected by the pseudonymous nyctodromist known as Awen.
## I'm Reading...
Awen posits that all humans experience the dream of eyes, but that only those aware of their ‘Sun-lineage’ are able to remember it. She believes this to be the reason why many accounts emerge from royal bloodlines, such as the Shadowless Kings and the Chrysanthemum Throne.
## I've Read...
Awen believes the Fifth Eye Curse and Winkwell to be corrupted iterations of the dream, which have persisted beyond their dreamers’ bodies. The Fourfold Princes attempted to prevent this by mummifying such dreamers, and could even access older dreams through them. She mourns their destruction by the conquistadors, regarded as brash and foolish instruments of Calyptra.

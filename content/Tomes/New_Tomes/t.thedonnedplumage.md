# The Donned Plumage
The Wayfinder Fasamixolesi writes about the ‘Un-Secret Secret’ of the Roost, by which certain gods-from-Nowhere may attend and contribute to their clandestine meetings.
## I'm Reading...
The Sister who is not the Sister and the Witch who is not the Witch sometimes take their place at meetings of the Roost. Perhaps the Kite-Twin is a bluebird in disguise, or a cuckoo which has learnt to mimic their song…
## I've Read...
Fasamixolesi suggests the Roost are aware of this masquerade and refuse to reveal it. She reasons that they wish to garner gossip only present in Nowhere, and that any non-aviform Hours are kept ignorant or apathetic through certain dealings of the Beach-Crow and the Ivory Dove.

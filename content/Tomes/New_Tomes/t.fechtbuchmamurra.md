# Fechtbuch Mamurra
One of the many fencing manuals which saturated Europe, notable for its decidedly unsanctioned techniques. This comes from the Corps Mamurra, a secretive fraternity of duelists said to commerce with old powers.
## I'm Reading...
Several techniques take after the spirits of the invisible world. ‘Use Hintering to evade a foe’s sight.’ ‘Employ the Bear-Beat to maintain momentum.’ ‘The Splint-Hew brings an assured end.’
## I've Read...
An incomplete chapter touches on the Worm-arts called Writhing. They can hone stray memories into Winning Moves through requisite Edge. Annotations mention how the application of Heart can turn fruit into a bracer against sluggishness, and alarmingly, allow one to speak with Worms.

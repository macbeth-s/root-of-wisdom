# Unspoken Roads
The Wayfinder Martin Olleta writes on the secret ways and places enfolded between and within the Wake, the Bounds, and the World-Spine.
## I'm Reading...
As a servant of the Sight-Thief, Olleta is charged with walking the ways others do not. ‘The road walked by no one is no road at all.’ He likens himself to a needle pulling thread, and makes references to the endless procession of the Hours through the World-Spine and the Wood.

# The Iron Legends
Erebor the Squall Hound relates what he believes to be ancient legends of the dragonslayers from his research in Eleia.
## I'm Reading...
The dragonslayers crusaded against ‘the serpents of stone’ in the Old World, and taught their ways to those who could harden their souls against the cries and allures of their quarries. 
## I've Read...
‘Dragonslayers of great renown could earn for themselves immortality,’ Erebor writes. He traces this idea to the Scarred Slayer whom they revered, whose own battle against the Seven-Coils granted him dominion as an Hour. ‘One only grows Long when another is no Longer, and the dragons were the Longest of us all…’

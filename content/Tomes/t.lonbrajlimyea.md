# Lonbraj Limye A  
Or “The Shadow of Light.” Madame Rogier of Port Noon bound this <i>livre d’art</i> herself. She sent nine copies to each Library of the Watchman’s Tree in the hopes of preserving them. Only two have been confirmed to reach their destination. \[This book houses a <i>numen</i> - a truth so powerful it can perhaps believe itself.]  
## I'm Reading...  
The gods-from-Light are subjects many artists have tried and failed to fully capture, but Rogier’s striking portraits surpass all prior attempts. Though the imagery is conventional, a few added details complete them in unexpected ways…  
## I've Read...  
Lithe arms in the Sun’s splendorous rays, a ruby hand beneath the Flowermaker’s treasures, and swollen masses at the sooted corners of the Forge reveal a secret kept in embers and twilight. Rogier credits her inspirations to her muse dwelling in the spring of oblivion.

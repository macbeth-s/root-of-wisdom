# The Hidden Light Sutra
This Sutra was authored by ‘One Sightless Who Sees’ who is perhaps an apostate Name, perhaps an unendorsed Long, or perhaps a mortal touched by the distant, glinting power known as Gleam.
## I'm Reading...
The Hidden Light Sutra exhorts the Shore-Thief, the Mirror-Queen, and the Lotus-Light not only as visionaries of possibility, but also as attendants or heralds to the Candle-Keeper. ‘Devarati carries fire in darkness; Devarati leaves what is not entered; Devarati turns our sight to gold.’
## I've Read...

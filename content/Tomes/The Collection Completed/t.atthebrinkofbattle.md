# At the Brink of Battle
Beatriz Huaylas contends that the existence of Edge predates the ascension of the Scarred Slayer. She wrote this after studying ‘the rites of curation’ practiced by the Cuetlaxtlapatl, the secretive order of midwives who delivered and oversaw the dynasties of the Triple Alliance.
## I'm Reading...

## I've Read...

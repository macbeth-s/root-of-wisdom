# The Recurrences of Doom
Eizi Fukuhara examines the deaths of the American Sun Kings - the Great Speaker of the Triple Alliance and the Highmost Prince of the Fourfold Realm - and the implications of their ends.
## I'm Reading...
Fukuhara proposes the murders of the Sun Kings presaged the Sun’s division, or even guaranteed its occurrence. ‘If the Fate of a King was to have his Time sundered, even the greatest of Kings would eventually follow; for the Past reflects the Future, as the Moon reflects the Sun.’
## I've Read...
Fukuhara expounds on how beautiful ends could in turn herald beautiful beginnings. He claims further insights could be found before a monument to the Sun-in-Rags, if one divined Auroral Contemplations with a nyctodromic memory.

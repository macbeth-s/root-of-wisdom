# One Beyond Sight
An anonymous work on the enigmatic Hour sometimes called the Wound, or the Diamond, which the author proposes is neither real nor unreal, but unrealized.
## I'm Reading...
‘No single title, to us in the West, can fully encapsulate his breadth; though in the Land of the River, the Hooded Princes name him the Giribrago.’
## I've Read...
The Giribrago is sometimes tied to the gods-from-stone, but remains elusive as to the nature of that relationship. The author indicates the ‘dragon brooders’, the Seven-Coils and the Egg Unhatching, are responsible for its current ‘ever-shedding form’.

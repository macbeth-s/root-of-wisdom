# El Corazón Ardiente
A manifesto from the legendary Comandante of the Shorn Brotherhood, the Warlock of the Andes, penned shortly before the end of the Saltpeter War. Ever since his disappearance in 1883, members of the Brotherhood have attempted to ascertain clues to his whereabouts in the text, to little success.
## I'm Reading...
The writing veers between revolutionary rhetoric and rapturous rambling, where the Warlock declares the Saltpeter War lost, but boasts of winning a great secret from the keepers of the Watchman’s Tree which can free mortalkind from ‘the iron chains of Death.’
## I've Read...
The utmost nature of Iotic Essence can come forth when sustained with sufficient Heart, creating a path to victory in the Fourth History. Though the Warlock acknowledges the difficulty of this, he nonetheless affirms its truth by declaring ‘both the Heart and the Flame begin and end in Red.’

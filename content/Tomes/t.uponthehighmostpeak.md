# Upon the Highmost Peak
On the requirements and duties of the Highmost Prince, ruler of the Fourfold Realm.
## I'm Reading...
‘The Highmost Prince must be a Know; the Highmost Prince must acknowledge every Hour; the Highmost Prince must stand apart, above, and between…’
## I've Read...
Every Highmost Prince pledged himself to one of the known Hours, who would define the direction of the Fourfold Realm during their reign. Nonetheless, the Prince was always likened to the Radiant Sun, the Realm itself considered a counterpart to the World-Spine where the Hours lived.

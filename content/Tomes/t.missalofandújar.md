# Missal of Andújar
The missal used at the Colegio de Santa Potenciana, which diverges greatly from the orthodox rites of the Church, even those shrouded rites unknown to the public.
## I'm Reading...
Successive rectors have modified it over the years. Initial changes emphasize the Meniscate as the highmost power of the House, even before she attained Hourhood. Later on, the Sun-in-Splendor and his other selves are disregarded entirely, ‘for though he is unconquered, his light remains unchanged.’
## I've Read...
‘When the moon comes before us, she may come as an ant or a bird or her sister; yet her face is always wounded. Let us pass by her light; let us pass in her darkness; let us pass into her House through her ever-exposed wound.’

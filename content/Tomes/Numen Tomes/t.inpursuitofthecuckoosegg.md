# In Pursuit of the Cuckoo's Egg  
Authored by Fabrosque of the Shorn Brotherhood, published at the Haustorium. Rarely does the Library issue external texts, but a ‘sumptuous friend,’ Robigo said, convinced him to take a chance. \[This book houses a <i>numen</i> - a truth so powerful it can perhaps believe itself.]  
## I'm Reading...  
‘Roots dig into History. Branches stretch for Eternity. In what direction does the Trunk grow? It does not reach out, but runs from its hollow heart. When the cuckoo passes its rings, it finds the matrix for its Egg…’

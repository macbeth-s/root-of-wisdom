# Book of the Fourfold Flower
A tome of dream-lore sourced from the Lower Princes of the First Dynasty, who favored foremost the Hours of the Wood.
## I'm Reading...
‘The House of the Sun is the Spine of the World. Above it lies the Glory. Beneath it is Nowhere. Around its walls - though it has no walls - is the Wood which hides it from the world, or the world from it. The ways to Nowhere are always sealed tight, but the roots of the Wood dig ever downwards…’
## I've Read...
‘Before the Sap-Spine drank the light of the Sun, she drank the light of the Moon. The Dreameater drinks moonlight now, but the old servants of the Sap-Spine who remember greener nights still yearn for softer light…’ The Book states that libations of those nights can be prepared from ‘the crowned coiled fruits’ with sufficient Nectar-aspect, but must only be given during the proper Hour of Renewal, ‘else the withered Histories be renewed.’

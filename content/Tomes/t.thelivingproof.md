# The Living Proof  
A thesis on geometry and geography, written and refined over the past three decades by ‘The Reciprocation’. \[This book houses a <i>numen</i> - a truth so powerful it can perhaps believe itself.]
## I'm Reading...  
The Bounds are the place where the waking world ends and another begins. Dreamers often cross them into the periphery of the Mansus, but the Reciprocation elaborates other possibilities for their paths…  
## I've Read...  
The Reciprocation outlines a process by which the geometry of the Bounds could be manipulated, and how other elements may be incorporated into its labyrinths. The explanations are quite detailed, though read far more like personal accounts than theoretical analyses.

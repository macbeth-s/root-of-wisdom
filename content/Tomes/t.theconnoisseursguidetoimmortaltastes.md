# The Connoisseur's Guide to Immortal Tastes
An expelled and disgraced member of the Tragulari discusses the taste and effects of the flesh of those Long he has slain.
## I'm Reading...
‘The flesh of Grail-long rejuvenates health, curing even the most tenacious diseases and granting longevity. Those remade by the Forge leave tough flesh and wanting flavor, but the marrow of their bones is unmatched in texture and tenderness.’
## I've Read...
The author prizes the Flowermaker-long as the most succulent of immortals, whose flesh invites sweet dreams and who bear the sweetest blood. He speaks highly of mellification, the gradual conversion of flesh into honey as the Long prepare to fully enter the Mansus and aid their patron Hour with his creations - either as assistants or as components for his work.

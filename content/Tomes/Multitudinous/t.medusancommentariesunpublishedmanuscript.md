# Medusan Commentaries (Unpublished Manuscript)  
Several copies of the Mutuality's discussions of Medusa's histories have been published, but even more radical unpublished editions have found their way into hidden collections around South America.  
## I'm Reading...  
Medusa came from the Seglaz-kind, bearing their wisdom and teeth. The Seven-Coils favored her, and even now, she carries his legacy; but it is only one of many. How many has she consumed? How many has she become?'  
## I've Read...  
The Haustorical Mutuality longed for a Ligean to join them, and desired Medusa most of all, for uncertain reasons. They are jealous over her ownership of the Forest Key; they sympathize with her imperceptible existance; they long to know the oldest secrets of the world; they wish to meet another who lives, yet is dead; they want to call her home.

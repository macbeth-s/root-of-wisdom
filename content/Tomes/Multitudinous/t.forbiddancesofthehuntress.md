# Forbiddances of the Huntress  
Certain codes of conduct set for Names, observed to appease and avoid the lethal attentions of the Huntress of Snows.  
## I'm Reading...  
Many of the gods-who-were-flesh, if not all, began their ascent as the Name of another Hour. Those Names-who-are know this well, and rarely, one gains the ambition for Hourhood themselves; but when the Huntress of Snows learns as such, she employs what she can to ensure they relent, in surrender or death...  
## I've Read...  
Seek to be a conduit, not a source. Should thou defect from thy master, enter the service of another. While the Madrugad stands, as does her Name; while her Name stands, her Silence shall fall. But if thou remains the seed in the earth, safe from the snow, when the Sun's light melts her once more, thy blooms shall burn and blossom bright!'

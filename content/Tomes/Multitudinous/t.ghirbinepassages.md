# Ghirbine Passages  
Fragmented chronicles on Ghirbi, Guardian of the Stag Door.  
## I'm Reading...  
Ghirbi was the first of the Know, amongst the company of mortals who shattered the Stag Door. As punishment, he was taken as a Name, condemned to grant others entrance to the Mansus, a right the Hours now deny him.  
## I've Read...  
It is said Ghirbi only speaks five riddles. But why five, and not seven? If we cannot to understand them, might we change their shape? If we lack the words to answer them, might we make our own?'

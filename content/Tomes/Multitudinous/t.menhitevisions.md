# Menhite Visions  
Revelatory proclamations received from Menhit, Name of the Lionsmith.  
## I'm Reading...  
Menhit smites her foes with glorious sunlight, and is considered by some to be one of his more monstrous Names, which may derive from her close ties to the primal earth, and the serpents which slumber therein.  
## I've Read...  
After the Lionsmith's legendary battle with Antaios, Menhit is said to have founded the first shrine to her master's worthy foe. Perhaps this is why so many who bear the Blood of the Earth take her as a patron.

# # The Flight of Time  
Subtitled 'The Wings of War'. A newsreel of murky origins, cryptically distributed to numerous cinemas, written in a language nearly no one understands.  
## I'm Reading...  
Reports on developments in the War in the World are interspersed with shots of stormy skies, roiling seas, rustling trees. All of these, according to the narrator, are signs of the other War to come.  
## I've Read...  
The film ends abruptly, and the credits roll. It is doubtful whether any of these people are real, but the sponsors stand out. Among them are the Crooky Trust Association, the White Husk Fraternity, and the Crossrow Arts League - all of which hold connections to the aviform Hours.

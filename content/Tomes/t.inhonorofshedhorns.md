# In Honor of Shed Horns
Various ‘horn songs’ performed by a wing of the Shorn Brotherhood located in Guaymas.
## I'm Reading...
They sing of the treasured sights of those who bear horns. The Horned Serpent seeks blood beneath the dry land; the Horned Owl splits the sky and finds a House behind; the Horned Moth hunts for light in the darkness of the Wood…
## I've Read...
As the songs continue, the words grow increasingly erratic, redoubled and archaic. Wilderness, flowers, enchantments, mountains; old sacrifices found above the earth, new sacrifices lost beneath the trees.; until all at once, it stills. And in the fragile silence afterwards, my skin seeks to soar.

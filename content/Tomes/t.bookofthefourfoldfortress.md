# Book of the Fourfold Fortress
A tome of dream-lore sourced from each of the Highmost Princes who ruled the Fourfold Realm from its juncture at the Rock of the Owl and therein acknowledged every Hour.
## I'm Reading...
‘The House of the Sun is the Spine of the World. Between its realms lie the Seven Doors, and each Door demands its proper sacrifice. The highest of these is the Eagle’s Gate, which grants immortality to those who pass. But the greatest is the Gate of Knowledge, which imparts upon its passers their rightful place beneath the Hours…’
## I've Read...
‘Before the Axe kept watch at all thresholds, she created them. The Diviner of Wounds creates them now, but the old servants of the Axe who remember deeper twilights still anticipate the splitting of the skies, the dripping of her blades…’ The Book states that libations of those twilights can be prepared from ‘the silver poison of dissolution’ with sufficient Knock-aspect, but must only be given during the proper Hour of Division, ‘else all Histories be divided.’

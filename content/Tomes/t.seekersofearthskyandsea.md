# Seekers of Earth, Sky, and Sea
Captain Tuponotanga on the Wayfinders, and of the wide and storied lineage from which they descend.
## I'm Reading...
Tuponotanga writes that the Wayfinders are one branch of the myriad clans of travelers and storytellers inspired by the wandering Hour called the Vagabond. He himself is part of these clans, one which works to allay the wounds from which History arises, and to prevent any unwelcome passage through these wounds. This led him to be present at the 1902 eruption of Mount Pelée, which he claims was caused by the ingression of a forbidden Hour…
## I've Read...
Tuponotanga bound the intrusion in ‘a spire-spine’ to prevent more drastic interventions from other Hours. In passing, he mentions how the Wayfinders used a similar technique to wield the earth’s might by feeding a Leashed Silence sufficient Scale, but stresses that their technique provided means to open new ways whereas his own mended them.

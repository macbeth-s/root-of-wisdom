# Those Who Walk the World
On the secretive order of travelers known as the Wayfinders, who touted themselves the keepers of the Histories.
## I'm Reading...
The Wayfinders claimed to know the hidden places and times of power, ever-traveling between them to ensure their continuance. Any outsiders who followed them would be lost in the wilderness, only led back to safety if they allowed their mind’s eye to be plucked out.
## I've Read...
Many rulers built exclusive sanctums for the Wayfinders in order to affix the legacies of their domains in the Histories. Following the Conquest, they are believed to have fled into the wilds to protect their secrets. Other accounts contend they fled to avoid vengeance, condemning them as traitorous guides to the conquistadors.

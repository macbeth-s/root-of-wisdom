# Dissections of the Soul
An overview of animatomy, the study of the elements of the soul, from Shoehi, a habitual scholar of the Yeshiva Tigris, the library of the Watchman’s Tree versed in matters of Preservation.
## I'm Reading...
Fet is the dream-self, tied by the silver cord to one’s wist, which holds the mortal self. The position of fet behind the navel leads Shohei to parallel it to the umbilical cord, the ‘serpent of birth’, and to consider whether every dream is ‘in some way, another birth.’
## I've Read...
‘Death is oft considered the cessation of health or chor, but any separation of the soul can be defined as death. In this sense, every Long is dead - perhaps every mortal-turned-immortal is, even those who are now Hours. But so long as the Glory burns, so long as Eternity is up and Death is down, they shall bear themselves up on the Breath of Life.’

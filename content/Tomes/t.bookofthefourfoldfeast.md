# Book of the Fourfold Feast
A tome of dream-lore sourced from the Upper Princes of the Second Dynasty, who favored foremost those Hours which dwelt high in the House of the Sun.
## I'm Reading...
‘The House of the Sun is the Spine of the World. At its heart lies the Chamber of Desires, where the Hours converge to realize their ambitions. At its crown lies the Red Temple, from which the Great Mother rules; and the Tomb of Worms, where the Scarred Lord keeps his quarry. The ways to the heights of the House demand blood, but such blood need not be one’s own…’
## I've Read...
‘Before the Mother drank the Tide, she drank from the blood of her worshippers. The Heart Relentless feeds her now, but the old servants of the Mother who remember redder days still recall her thirst for the blood of the Sun…’ The Book states that libations of those days can be prepared from ‘the crimson blood of flowers’ with sufficient Grail-aspect, but must only be given during the proper Hour of Birth, ‘else an untamed History be birthed.’

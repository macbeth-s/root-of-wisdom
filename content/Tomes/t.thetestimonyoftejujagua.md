# The Testimony of Teju Jagua
Fray Xavier Saavedra recorded this from a Guarani veteran of the War of Seven Reductions he encountered during his travels along the Peabiru.
## I'm Reading...
The first peoples who lived in the Amazon struggled against the monsters which nested there. The ‘unhuman apostle’ Tumé came to teach humanity how these monsters may be subjugated, in recognition of the service their ‘wise kin’ performed for his own ‘Scion Yet Unnamed’.
## I've Read...
Prophesying the arrival of invaders from the East, Tumé reveals the existence of a hidden mountain sanctuary, a refuge for mortals and monsters. He speaks of ‘the forbidden means of persistence’ which remain there, and of the last ‘coil-children’ for whom it was first raised…

# Seven Sins and Salvations
The Unsaint Amadea’s meditations on the redemptive nature of wounds, which Fray Silas Menard treasured for awakening his holy calling.
## I'm Reading...
‘Anything may leave a wound, and a wound may contain anything. He who contains many wounds and holds the world’s sins within thereby makes all the world most holy.’
## I've Read...
Amadea outlines the <i>stigmata ecstases</i>, wounds may open under the auspices of the seven principles to create a way between the waking world and the realms of dream. The Doors of the Mansus are exalted as the <i>stigmata sacra</i> of secret knowledge, but she advises prudence in passing them. ‘Every choice closes a door, and the Mansus has only seven.’

# Recipes from the Grove of Immortality
Professor Ji Yuan’s gastronomic work exploring ‘forbidden cuisines’ developed by the Green Sages.
## I'm Reading...
Few of these recipes are practical to cook, let alone eat. Many ingredients are exclusively sourced from the Sages’ gardens, and more applicable to creating poultices, poisons, or both.
## I've Read...
Obliquely described is the Vitulation, a ceremonial feast by which a Grail-long may ascend to Namehood. It requires the preparation of the Seven Graces, a bespoke set of spices, to induce sufficient hungers. Yuan notes that similar hungers may be induced in a coil-remnant with sufficient Nectar-aspect to ripen it into a ‘gnarled sweetness.’

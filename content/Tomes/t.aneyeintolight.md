# An Eye Into Light
The ocularist Catalina Messalina expounds on the occult properties of glass and their alterations by certain minerals, which she believes to be fallen shards of the Egg Unhatching, shed during his flight to the Glory.
## I'm Reading...
‘Obsidian, stone burnt to light. Lime, memory drowned to time. Cinnabar, fire cooled to blood. These are not glass entire, but let light leak through them, and watch what wakes in their shadows.’
## I've Read...
Messalina seeks to create from these shards a glass eye capable of glimpsing what the Egg saw in the Glory in his final moments, hoping to earn a place in the Vagabond’s Pilgrimage into the Light above the House. She mentions how she incidentally synthesized the Ichor Vitreous from glass via Sky aspect, and believes she may be approaching ‘the conjunction of Mercy and Truth’.

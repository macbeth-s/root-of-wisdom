# The Skin of Monsters
A tale of the ‘second ascension’ of the Hour known as the Lionsmith, by the Limian and horomachist Melerai Yivni.
## I'm Reading...
Melerai views the Lionsmith, the ‘Untarnished Valiant’, with pity and sympathy: for his centuries of loyalty to the Chiliarch, for his faith in his mentor’s promises, and for the yearnings long smothered in deference to his progenitor.
## I've Read...
The Valiant enters the Wood seeking the assistance of untoward powers. The fire in his heart wins him the capricious love of the Moth and the Mare, and they gift him the Skin of Monsters. Donning it, he enters the Mansus through the Savage Door, undertaking a new ascension outside of the Chiliarch’s control.

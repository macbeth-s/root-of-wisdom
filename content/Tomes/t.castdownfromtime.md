# Cast Down from Time
An exposition on Hours belonging ‘neither to Mansus, nor Wood, nor Wake, nor Nowhere’; by the Damascene Lethean and skolekosophist Para, son of Aaron.
## I'm Reading...
Three Hours were banished by their peers, with memories of them and even the reasons for their banishment suppressed… but Para theorizes their principal aspects remained. ‘Who gave the Knock? Why must Winter wait? From whence did the Edge arise?’
## I've Read...

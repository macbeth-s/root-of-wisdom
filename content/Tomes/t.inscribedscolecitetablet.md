# Inscribed Scolecite Tablet
An impossible tablet of worm-stone, formed such that the acicular fibers form writing in Killasimi.<br>Initial Location: [[fourfoldsacrarium|Fourfold Sacrarium]]
## I'm Reading...
‘Humanity hid its most forbidden secrets in the Roots of the Watchman's Tree. The Wise knew to stay in the Glory's Light, but we sought knowledge and found in the darkness our deepest selves.'
## I've Read...
The text reads as a covenant-stone, the token which gives a library shelter under the Watchman's Tree. It concludes by speaking of the Haustorium's mission: to pursue knowledge no matter how foul, how rejected, how misunderstood, and to find greatness therein.

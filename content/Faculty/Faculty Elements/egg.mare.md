# Vermillion Egg
It is no larger than an ostrich egg, no warmer than a tea-kettle. Shura has secreted it here through a connection with the Nowhere-Hour called the Mare in the Tree. It must be held at arms length, with oven mitts. \[Shura has asked you to nestle their future child into the cradle so that it might hatch safely.]

## Recipes
### Lower the Key
`upgrade.angstloch.void.key`
<br>**Requirements:** [[library.angstloch|Angstloch]], void.key
<br>**Start Description:** The key perches above the void below, then descends.

<br>**Effects:** -1 void.key, Replace [[library.angstloch|Angstloch]] with [[library.angstloch.key|Shura's Hidey-Hole]]

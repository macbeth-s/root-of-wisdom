# Dottore's Saddle
'<i>Helvella contenta</i> is among the most important of species available for academic review. It has been identified as a potent intoxicant, a producer of visions, a hearty meal best paired with robust meat, and a reminder of the crowning accomplishment of life represented by fungi. Herein I provide other notes about how it might be beneficially cultivated in any place where there are sufficient nutrients in the soil...' –Lacombe, Charles. 'Towards the Culmination of Mycological Study,' 1940, under review for publication.
## Consider
A meal so filling one feels as if they had partaken of a thousand feasts from centuries past. The sensation spreads from gut to heart, from heart to head.

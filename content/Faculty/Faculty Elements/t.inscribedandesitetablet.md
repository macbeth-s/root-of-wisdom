# Inscribed Andesite Tablet
The true, or perhaps just the first, Covenant-Stone of the Haustorium–the token which gives the Library shelter under the Watchman's Tree.
## I'm Reading...
‘Humanity heard the Law of the Chancel, the limits of the Calyptra, and fled to the roots where they could live as they pleased, and love as they wished. Their skin was soft but their eyes wide and in that danger they found safety in the Watchman's Tree.'
## I've Read...
The Tablet expounds on the virtues of those who seek knowledge, who refuse the limits of Law or taboo, and who someday–maybe–may find peace despite what they have done in the name of greatness.

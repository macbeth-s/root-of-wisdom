# Unrealized Axiom
A 'gift of Light' granted by I Who Still-Did-Will Continue. Here is knowledge as a mote of light, but its true form is not yet known. \[I Who Still-Did-Will Continue asked you to place it in the Mirror Hold or the Burning Mirror; or you could refuse this gift by granting it a beautiful end in the appropriate place.]
## Aspects
- #Memory 
- #Persistent
- #Light
## Recipes
### Realize a Gift of the Sun
`upgrade.mirrors.axiom`
<br> **Requirements:** axiom.unrealized, [[library.mirrors|Mirrors]]
<br>**Start Description:** The mote takes many shapes in the manifold reflections. Then they begin to resolve into one.
<br>**Description:** Every mirror shines brighter, each reflection more glorious than the last. Light haloes my face and my visage is more perfect. A promise of delight from a principle to be - Corona. \[The Mirrors now admit Grail intentions. They also provide Grail aspect.]
<br>**Effects:** -1 axiom.unrealized, Replace [[library.mirrors|Mirrors]] with [[library.mirrors.axiom|Golden Mirrors]]

### Realize a Gift of the Stars
`upgrade.mirror.burning.axiom`
<br> **Requirements:** axiom.unrealized, [[library.mirror.burning|Burning Mirror]]
<br>**Start Description:** The mote wavers on the glass, dancing like a dying flame. Then the sparks begin to rise.
<br>**Description:** Higher they fly, burning hues growing smaller until they are as Stars in the sky. One-by-one they wink out, but in this I glimpse a message. An assurance of opportunity from a principle to be - Gleam. \[The Burning Mirror now admits Rose intentions. It also provides Rose Aspect.]
<br>**Effects:** -1 axiom.unrealized, Replace [[library.mirror.burning|Burning Mirror]] with [[library.mirror.burning.axiom|Gleaming Mirror]]

### Realize a Gift of the Future
`upgrade.cairn.axiom`
<br> **Requirements:** axiom.unrealized, [[library.cairn|Cairn Intercalate]]
<br>**Start Description:** The mote shimmers incessantly, but now its rays begin to slow. Is the skull smiling at me?
<br>**Description:** The light stills to a sculpture of illuminated glass. For a moment it is beautiful, then it shatters to diamond dust. Gently it settles over the cairn. The eye-hollows of the skull fill with an avid light. \[The Cairn now admits Edge and Moth intentions.]
<br>**Effects:** -1 axiom.unrealized, Replace [[library.cairn|Cairn Intercalate]] with [[library.cairn.axiom|Avid Cairn Intercalate]]

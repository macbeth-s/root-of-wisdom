# Robigo, Engorged
It is remarkable how their flesh may change and their skin may stretch. To eat truly is to become. \[While Robigo visits in this form, they will expect to be well-fed and taken care of. Otherwise, they may take out their frustration on your persistent memories.]
## Aspects
- [[protector|Protector]]
- [[Faculty]]
## Description of Mechanic
When this form of Robigo is drawn, a verb with a 20 second timer will begin grabbing persistent memories unless the proper food or beverage is provided, consuming up to 12 persistent memories over the course of a season of [[celestial_recipes_time##Camai Quilla|Camai Quilla]].

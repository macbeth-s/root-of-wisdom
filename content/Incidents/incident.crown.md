# The Affair of the Pale Sovereign
The oracle-painter Rita Vernyhora has befuddled the invisible world with her unveiling of ‘The Pale Sovereign’ in Kyiv. Many agree it depicts an Hour; few agree on which; all are concerned that she dubbed it a funerary portrait.

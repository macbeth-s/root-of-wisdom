# Moth, the Spider's Prey
The Moth hunts in the wood, the Moth will not be satisfied, the Moth seeks change. The Rising Spider shares all but the last of these, and for this she hates the Moth. She will ensnare him in her webs, and from his dapple-blood she will become the one who chooses how we change, and when, and why. \[This determination is made with moth; it provides moth and moon.]

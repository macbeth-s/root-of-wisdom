# Lime, the Hatched Egg
In the time of Stone, the Egg Unhatching protected our existence when all other Hours slept and forgot. It has hatched, but the lime shell remains; in the Dawn, in the Snow, in the Walls. It shields us from the Glory which would engulf our lives. Age is the shelter of mortals. \[This determination is made with sky; it provides sky and winter.

# Gold, the Color of Blood
The Golden Blood of the Seven-Coils was the first to be spilt, and the source from which the Histories draw. It was the gnashing of her many necks that spilt blood on the seven strands, that brought them together in coils, that make the severed few still approach. \[This determination is made with edge; it provides edge and knock.]

# Red, the Color of Blood
The Tide was night-blue, but the Grail drew from it gouts of decadent red which the Histories have lapped at gleefully. It is our blood which feeds the Histories, but it is the kind and cruel thirsting of the Grail and the Mother that conspires always to see their persistence. \[This determination is made with nectar; it provides nectar and grail.]

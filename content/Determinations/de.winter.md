# Dove, the Spider's Prey
The Ivory Dove remembers, and through those memories he offers another way. The Rising Spider wishes for only one ascent, one way: through her webs. She sets her plans in motion and she waits for the Dove's quiet wings so that she might own the ways of remembering and in doing so let us remember only her. \[This determination is made with winter; it provides winter and scale.]

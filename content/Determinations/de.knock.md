# Vak, the Spider's Prey
Vak is a beautiful goddess, an exception, Calyptra's great lackey and great weakness. The Rising Spider weaves her web behind many doors, but the Peacock Door alone she wishes to ensnare, so that the ways of coming and going, and the ways of Knowing and Unknowing, might be hers. \[This determination is made with knock; it provides knock and rose.]

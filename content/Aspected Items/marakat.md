# Marakat
The <i>encaustum terminale</i> of the Fifth History. The colour of the Principle that once occupied the place that Heart does now. In the Fifth History itself, the cult of Marakat was everywhere suppressed, but suppression has never bothered the librarians of the Haustorium. \[Use an Encaustum ink with your Journal to make Determinations and record Histories. If the Hours listen, the world changes... Robigo is also known to answer invitations written in Marakat with an offer to help, if you can provide at least 20 of \<sprite name=grail> and your writing-case.]
